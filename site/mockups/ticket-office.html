01 · Short-lived

Every ticket expires

Sandboxes are deleted when the command ends, and none outlives its time limit. --timeout 10m stops a runaway command.

02 · Locked down

Nothing leaves unstamped

The VM has no network card. Traffic goes through a proxy that allows only the hosts you list and logs every attempt.

03 · Signed

You keep the stub

The host signs a record of commands, connections and the deleted disk. ephemera verify checks it.
