Every sandbox leaves evidence.

ephemera runs your dev servers, tests and coding agents in microVMs that start in 65 ms and are deleted when they finish. Outbound traffic is blocked unless you allow it, and the host signs a record of everything that happened.

Short-lived

Nothing stays at the scene

The VM and its disk are deleted when the command ends. Every sandbox has a time limit, and --timeout stops a runaway command.

Locked down

No way out you didn't allow

The VM has no network card. Its only route out is a proxy that allows the hosts you list and logs every attempt.

Signed

A record that holds up

Commands, connections and the deleted disk, signed with the host's Ed25519 key. ephemera verify checks it.
